Privacy

This page explains, in plain terms, how this website handles your information.

  • The website collects the customer details you enter into the checkout form so that an order enquiry can be prepared.
  • You are then redirected to WhatsApp to send your order. You must press Send in WhatsApp for ForYouApple to receive it.
  • The website does not process payments. Payment arrangements are confirmed through WhatsApp.
  • This static website does not use a customer account database.
  • Cart information may be stored locally in your browser using localStorage, so your cart is still there when you return. You can clear it at any time by removing items or clearing your browser data.

This website does not include analytics or tracking scripts.

Questions about your information? Contact ForYouApple through WhatsApp or at marvelofficialmail@gmail.com.
